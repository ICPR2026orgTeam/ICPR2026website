Getting Around Lyon with TCL

Zones 1 and 2 cover the conference area and the principal ICPR venues.
Tickets are valid on TCL buses, trams, metro and funiculars within the zones purchased.

Fares shown below were checked in August 2026. Validate your ticket or bank card at the beginning of every journey and again at each connection.

Single journey

€2.10 zones 1–2

Valid for one hour from the first validation, including connections between bus, tram, metro and funicular services. The ticket must be validated again at every connection.

Reusable paper ticket

The reloadable paper ticket costs €0.20. Keep it: it can be reloaded at a TCL ticket machine with single journeys, a bundle of 10 journeys or compatible passes.

Pay with a bank card

Tap a contactless bank card directly on the validator. Multiple journeys made with the same card are automatically capped at €6.90 per day for zones 1–2.

The 7-day pass can be loaded onto the €0.20 reusable paper ticket; visitors do not need to purchase a personal travel card. Validity begins with the first validation.

Tribu day pass

€7.00 for one group

Unlimited travel for one day for a group that remains together. The group may be:

  • one adult with one to four minors aged 0–17; or
  • two adults with one to three minors aged 0–17.

At the first journey and at every connection, validate the ticket once for each person in the group. The Tribu pass is not available through contactless bank-card payment.

Organised groups

One-hour group journey

For 10 or more people travelling together: €1.70 per person, instead of €2.10. Purchase through a TCL agency.

One-day adult group ticket

For 11 or more adults: €3.40 per person for one day. Group members may travel together or separately. This product must be ordered in advance through TCL's administrative/group channel rather than a standard ticket machine.
