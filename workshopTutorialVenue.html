How to Reach Workshops & Tutorials

Workshops and tutorials: 21–22 August 2026. The main conference on 17–20 August is held at a different location.

Louis Néel Building

INSA Lyon — Bâtiment Louis Néel
07 Avenue Jean Capelle
69621 Villeurbanne, France

Option 1 — Tram T1

  1. Take tram T1 towards IUT Feyssine.
  2. Get off at Croix-Luizet.
  3. The Louis Néel Building is directly opposite the tram stop.

Option 2 — Tram T4 or T6

  1. Take tram T4 or T6 to La Doua–Gaston Berger.
  2. From there, walk approximately 15 minutes to the Louis Néel Building, or change to tram T1 towards IUT Feyssine for two stops.
