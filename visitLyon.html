Visit Lyon

Discover the historic heart of Lyon with a guided tour of the Vieux Lyon district.

We are pleased to offer two guided walking tours during ICPR 2026, taking place on Tuesday, August 18 and Thursday, August 20, 2026. Each tour lasts approximately two hours and is limited to 25 participants.

For more information about the heritage and architecture covered during the tours, see the Visit Lyon tour description.
