Install the ICPR 2026 App

You can install the ICPR 2026 website on your phone and use it like an app. No app store is required.

Android

  1. Open the ICPR 2026 website in Google Chrome.
  2. Open the browser menu using the three dots in the top-right corner.
  3. Select Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm the installation.
  5. The ICPR 2026 icon will appear on your home screen or in your app drawer.

If available, choose Install app rather than creating a simple shortcut.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Open the ICPR 2026 website in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Scroll down and select Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.
  5. The ICPR 2026 icon will appear on your home screen.

On iPhone and iPad, installation must be done from Safari.

Enable notifications

After installing the app, you can enable notifications from the ICPR 2026 website to receive important conference announcements and reminders.
