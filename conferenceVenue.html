How to Reach the Conference Venue

Main conference: 17–20 August 2026. Workshops and tutorials on 21–22 August are held at a different location.

Lyon Convention Centre

Lyon Convention Centre
Esplanade Raymond Barre
69006 Lyon, France
Main entrance Please use the Amphitheatre B entrance, located behind the orange “Man on the Phone” statue.

By bus

The easiest option is the high-frequency C1, C5 or C23 bus to Cité Internationale – Centre de Congrès, the closest stop to the entrance.

The C2 and C26 buses stop at Cité Internationale – Transbordeur, a short walk from the Convention Centre.

From Lyon Part-Dieu railway station, take the C1 towards Cuire for a direct connection.

C1 C2 C5 C23 C26

By metro and on foot

From Masséna station on metro line A, the venue is approximately a 30-minute walk through Parc de la Tête d'Or and across the Rhône-side entrance to Cité Internationale.
